# Tree textures

Downloaded from the ez-tree demo app (github.com/dgreenheck/ez-tree @ dcf309b) and shrunk to 512².

- `leaf_*.png`: part of ez-tree, MIT licence (see src/decorations/lib/ez-tree/LICENSE).
- `bark_*.jpg`: the Color maps of ambientCG Bark001/002/003, CC0 1.0 (public domain).
  Sources: https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Bark001 (and Bark002, Bark003).
